# 🎮 The Forgotten House - Play Store Optimization Guide

## 📋 IMPORTANT: Upload These Assets to Google Play Console

This guide will help you optimize your Play Store listing for maximum visibility and conversions.

---

## 🎯 CRITICAL: Missing Assets You Must Upload

### 1. **App Icon (REQUIRED)**
- **Size**: 512x512 pixels
- **Format**: PNG
- **Background**: Transparent recommended
- **Design**: Should feature your game's logo/branding
- **Suggestion**: Use your "✦ The Forgotten House" branding

### 2. **Feature Graphic (REQUIRED)**
- **Size**: 1024x500 pixels
- **Format**: PNG or JPEG
- **Purpose**: Appears at the top of your store listing
- **Design**: Show game title, main character/location, and "Free" or "New" badge

### 3. **Screenshots (REQUIRED - Minimum 2)**
**Recommendation**: Upload 6-8 screenshots showing:
1. **Title Screen** - Game name and branding
2. **Main Gameplay** - Hidden object scene with items to find
3. **Puzzle Solving** - Show a lock or code being solved
4. **Story Context** - Dialogue or journal entries
5. **Different Locations** - Manor, Boathouse, Sanatorium
6. **Unique Features** - X-ray screen, mirrors, special mechanics
7. **Settings/Progress** - Show saving, hints, journal
8. **Call to Action** - "Download Now" or "Start Your Investigation"

**Teknikal Requirements**:
- **Portrait**: 1080x1920 pixels (for phones)
- **Landscape**: 1920x1080 pixels (for tablets) 
- **Format**: PNG or JPEG
- **Maximum**: 8 screenshots per orientation

### 4. **Promotional Video (HIGHLY RECOMMENDED)**
- **Length**: 30-60 seconds
- **Format**: MP4 or WebM
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 or higher
- **Content**: Show actual gameplay, not just cinematics
