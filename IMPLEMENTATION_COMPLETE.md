## 💡 **PRO TIPS FOR YOUR PLAY STORE ASSETS**

### **Icon Design Tips:**
- Keep it simple and recognizable at small sizes
- Use your game's primary branding
- Test on different background colors
- Make sure it looks good at 48×48 (smallest size seen in app drawers)

### **Screenshot Tips:**
- First screenshot should show your game's title/main branding
- Show actual gameplay, not just menus
- Use captions to highlight key features
- Maintain consistent visual style across all screenshots
- Include "beta" or "new" badges if applicable

### **Video Tips:**
- First 5 seconds are most important - grab attention
- Show actual gameplay, not just cinematics
- Use captions (many users watch without sound)
- End with a clear call-to-action ("Download Now")
